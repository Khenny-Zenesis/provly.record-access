Why each index exists:
  - "Record_userId_publicId_key" UNIQUE (userId, publicId) — the ownership/access index. The
    detail + delete path resolves directly by (userId, publicId) in one query; a record can
    never be addressed without its owner (R4.1/R4.5/R4.9).
  - "Record_userId_createdAt_idx" (userId, createdAt DESC) — the list query
    `WHERE userId=$1 ORDER BY createdAt DESC` is answered straight from the index ordering.
  - AuditLog actorUserId index — "this actor's trail, newest first".
